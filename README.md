### Featured projects

#### 📊 [Sector Pulse: jobs, stocks, careers and live job postings by sector](https://github.com/resulisik/california-jobs-by-sector) · [live dashboard](https://resulisik.github.io/california-jobs-by-sector/)

California vs the U.S., 2021–2026. Combines 34 BLS payroll series, 11 S&P 500 sector funds, Indeed postings, BLS employment projections, New York Fed outcomes by college major, and a one-day snapshot of **39,215 job postings** collected from the public job APIs of 283 employers, all in a 19-table SQLite database.

- **43 SQL queries** (CTEs, window functions, multi-source joins); a validation script checks the results against published state and federal figures
- Tested keyword rules that sort postings by job family, seniority and location
- Findings: health care produced 61% of California's net new jobs; only 10% of live postings are entry level; 77% of data postings name SQL

`Python` `pandas` `SQL` `SQLite` `Matplotlib` `HTML/JavaScript`

#### ⚽ [FIFA World Cup 2026: performance and value analysis](https://github.com/resulisik/fifa2026-analysis)

A Python pipeline over 100 matches and 5,012 player-match records. A regression of player rating on market value ranks over- and under-performing players and teams; three 4-3-3 squads are built under budget limits; 10 SQL queries; slide decks in English and Turkish.

`Python` `pandas` `scikit-learn` `Seaborn` `SQL` `Jupyter`
